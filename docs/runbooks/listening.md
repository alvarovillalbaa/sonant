# Listening protocol

Last updated: 2026-10-15

Use a comfortable volume. Audition every character on laptop speakers, phone speakers, and headphones. Compare paired actions and all outcomes. Stop when fatigued; do not normalize perceived quality from a single listening session.

Exercise realistic workflows: a successful save, recoverable failure, rapid selections, a cancelled operation, a long request that hits its watchdog, a route change, backgrounding, mute/restoration, and speech suppression. Confirm every sound has an understandable silent equivalent.

Record device, browser/version, output device, volume, background media, cue or sequence, and observations. Distinguish “the API accepted playback” from “the listener heard it.” Record clipping, irritation, masked cues, abrupt stops, or confusing semantics as findings.

This release's deterministic checks do not measure LUFS, true peak, human distinctiveness, comprehension, retention, or task completion. Professional sound design, assistive-technology review, and real-device listening remain manual validation.
